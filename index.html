NexusID

Registry Infrastructure

One ID.
Every Tournament.

NexusID is a participation record for grassroots esports. Tournament organizers log results under your Nexus ID, so your history travels with you across events, teams, and organizers — instead of starting over every time.

How It Works

01
Create ID

A tournament organizer creates your Nexus ID and logs your placement and kills for each match.

02
Record Persists

Your record follows your in-game UID, not your display name — so it survives IGN changes and team switches.

03
Anyone Can Look Up

Anyone can look up a Nexus ID to see a player's or organizer's tournament history.
